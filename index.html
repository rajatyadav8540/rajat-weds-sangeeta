Day 1 · Monday 7 December 2026

Tilak

तिलक समारोह

The celebrations begin at home. The bride's family comes with a thali of kumkum, rice and sweets to bless the groom and welcome him into the family.

  • Monday, 7 December
  • Vill. Bhadeiyi, Post Kumhawar, Etawah

Day 2 · Wednesday 9 December 2026

Haldi

हल्दी की रस्म

Turmeric paste, marigold showers and the first dance of the week. Everyone goes home a little yellow, especially the bride and groom.

  • Wednesday, 9 December
  • Vill. Bhadeiyi, Post Kumhawar, Etawah

Day 3 · Friday 11 December 2026

Baaraat

बारात आई है

Rajat rides in on his decorated ghodi, with the band, the dhol and the lights leading the way to the mandap. Join the procession and dance the whole way there.

  • Friday, 11 December
  • Balaji Resort, Bharthan, Etawah

Day 4 · Saturday 12 December 2026

Phere

& Vidaai

सात फेरे, सात वचन · विदाई

Under the flower-decked mandap, as panditji chants the mantras, Rajat and Sangeeta, tied together by the gathbandhan, walk around the sacred fire seven times, one vow for every round.

  1. To nourish each other
  2. To grow strong together
  3. To share all prosperity
  4. To honour both families
  5. To raise a loving home
  6. To stay together through every season
  7. To be friends for life

Then, with blessings and a few tears, comes the vidaai.

  • Saturday, 12 December
  • Balaji Resort, Bharthan, Etawah
